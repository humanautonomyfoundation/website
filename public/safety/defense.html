What follows

Three lessons from the stack Our analysis

Model-level defenses can be undone

Training-time safety is shallow and can be fine-tuned away, and a model can be tampered with so that no test reveals a hidden capability. System-level layers such as containment, provenance and monitoring are what remain when the model itself can't be trusted.

Containment covers the most threats

Least privilege, sandboxes and information-flow control rate at least partial against every threat in the matrix, because they limit damage without needing to know why the model misbehaved. The UK AISI incident is the cautionary case: agents had internet access by design.

Evals are the weakest link that everything leans on

Release decisions and safety cases rest on evaluations, yet evals are the layer most exposed to test awareness, sandbagging and tampering. That is why making evals predict real failures ranks first in our research priorities.
