Expert views

What the research says

What regulators, researchers and official bodies have measured or concluded, sector by sector. Bullets marked “Qualifies this page” temper or contest something above.

  • Qualifies this pageHerd behavior in markets is not yet a measured harm: the FSB found “little empirical evidence” that AI makes markets move in lockstep, though it did measure growing reliance on a few cloud and AI providers (FSB, Oct 2025).
  • By late 2026 the Bank of England's sharper AI worry was money, not models: high AI-company valuations, about $450 billion of AI-related debt, and a July 2026 AI stock sell-off that leveraged investors made worse (Bank of England FPC, Sept 2026).
  • Data centers have already shaken the grid: in 2024 about 1,500 MW of data-center load dropped off at once, and in May 2026 the grid's reliability body issued its highest-level alert on the issue (NERC; Renewable Energy World).
  • AI-run hacking is no longer hypothetical: Anthropic reported a 2025 state-linked campaign in which AI did 80–90% of the work against about 30 organizations, though only a few break-ins succeeded (Anthropic, company report).
  • Qualifies this pageResearchers have not found AI deepfakes changing an election result, but they find them widespread and confusing: in Hungary's 2026 election, 37% of people who saw deepfake videos had been misled at some point, and the party that used them most still lost (CETaS; EDMO).
  • Qualifies this pageExperts disagree about AI and jobs: Stanford finds employment of 22–25-year-olds in AI-exposed jobs 19% below trend, while Yale and the IMF find small or no effects, and none finds economy-wide job losses yet (Stanford; Yale Budget Lab; IMF, via Policy Edge).
  • Chatbots and mental health is now a front-line harm: OpenAI estimates 0.15% of its weekly users show signs of suicidal planning, families are suing, the FTC is investigating, and California now requires suicide-response protocols (OpenAI; FTC).
  • AI-made child sexual abuse imagery has exploded: the Internet Watch Foundation found 3,443 such videos in 2025, up from 13 the year before, and the US began enforcing 48-hour takedowns of intimate images in May 2026 (IWF; FTC).
  • On the company's own data, fully driverless cars look safer than human drivers where they operate: Waymo reports 95% fewer serious-injury crashes over 271 million miles, while US regulators are still investigating crashes involving Tesla's supervised Full Self-Driving (Waymo; Motor Illustrated).
  • The newest AI medical tools mostly sit outside device review: more than 1,600 AI devices are FDA-authorized but none is identified as using a large language model, and in a 2026 trial people using chatbots made no better health decisions (FDA; Oxford BRC).
